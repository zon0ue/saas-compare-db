Where does the pricing data on this site come from?

All pricing data comes directly from the official pricing pages of the 11 tools tracked here (MailerLite, GetResponse, ActiveCampaign, Kit, AWeber, Omnisend, Brevo, Moosend, Sender, Klaviyo, beehiiv). Every price is shown in US dollars, as published by the vendor. Promotional prices, such as Black Friday offers, are not listed as regular prices.

How often are prices checked, and how can I see when a price was last verified?

Prices are re-checked on a weekly cycle. Each product page shows its last-checked date next to the link to the official pricing page it came from, and the changelog at the bottom of the page records what was verified.

How is the "annual billing (per month)" price calculated?

It is the per-month equivalent of paying for a full year up front, as published by the vendor on its pricing page. We record the figure the vendor publishes; we do not estimate an annual rate that a vendor has not published.

Why are some prices shown as "Not confirmed"?

A price is shown as "Not confirmed" when the vendor does not publish that value on its official pricing page, and we never fill in a missing figure by estimating. For example, beehiiv's pricing page showed only an annual-billing figure, so its monthly price is listed as not confirmed.

What is the difference between a free plan and a free trial?

A free plan is ongoing and does not expire, but it has published limits — for example, MailerLite's free plan covers 250 subscribers and Kit's covers 10,000 subscribers. A free trial is time-limited access to a paid product: ActiveCampaign has no free plan and offers a 14-day trial, and Moosend has no free plan and offers a 30-day trial.

Why can't I compare the entry prices in the table directly?

Vendors bill on different bases. Most of the tools here price by contacts or subscribers, while Brevo prices by monthly send volume, so a row priced at 500 subscribers and a row priced at 1,000 contacts are not measuring the same thing. Prices also do not scale linearly with list size, which is why we never extrapolate a price for a list size a vendor has not published.

A tool I use just changed its price. When will this site reflect it?

The weekly check picks up changes on vendors' official pricing pages. When a change is found, the product page is updated and the change is recorded in the changelog at the bottom of that product page, with the date it was checked.

Are the rankings influenced by affiliate commissions?

No. Rankings on the "best for" pages are computed mechanically from the recorded pricing data, and commission rates do not influence rankings or product order. Some links on this site are affiliate links; that is disclosed in the PR box near the top of every page.

Do you test the products yourselves?

No. This site does not hands-on test these products and makes no claim of personal experience with them. Everything published here is based on official pricing data published by the vendors.

Does annual billing always save money, and how much?

Where a vendor publishes both a monthly price and an annual per-month price, the annual figure is the monthly price with a discount applied for paying a year up front, so it is lower per month in exchange for the longer commitment. In our current data, for every tool where both a monthly and an annual price are confirmed, the annual per-month price is lower — by 10.0% to 30.0% at the entry tier.
