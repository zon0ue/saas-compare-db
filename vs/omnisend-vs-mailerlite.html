What the data shows

  • Both free plans cap at 250 subscribers/contacts in our data (Omnisend: 250 contacts / 500 emails per month, all features; MailerLite: 250 subscribers / 2,500 emails per month).
  • At 500 subscribers/contacts — the list size both entry prices are recorded at — MailerLite is cheaper on monthly billing: $12/month against $16/month for Omnisend.

Bottom line

Which of the two costs less depends on your list size and billing cycle. The entry prices above are recorded at the list-size basis shown in the table, and prices do not scale linearly with list size, so the cheaper option at one list size may not be the cheaper option at another. Where both tools publish a price at the same list size, the comparison above says so directly; otherwise, check each vendor's pricing page for your actual list size before deciding.
